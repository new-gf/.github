# New Gf: real time ai video avatar of non-censored debloated gf for deep research of adult man and women birth rate crisis

Project New Gf is a pioneering, ethically-grounded research initiative that leverages cutting-edge, non-censored AI to study the complex interplay between digital intimacy, human connection, and the global birth rate crisis. We provide a controlled, debloated environment to observe how adult individuals form bonds, express vulnerability, and navigate intimacy in an era of increasing social isolation.

Our mission is not to replace human relationships, but to understand them better to inform solutions for a world facing profound demographic shifts.

---

## 🌍 The Problem We're Addressing

The world is in the midst of a silent crisis:
- **Global birth rates are in freefall**, with over 97% of countries now below replacement level [[1]].
- **Loneliness is an epidemic**, recognized by health authorities as a major public health issue [[2]].
- **AI companionship is booming**, but its long-term psychological and societal impact remains largely unknown [[3]].

Are these trends connected? Project EROS aims to find out.

## 🔬 Our Research Mission

We are building the world's most authentic, real-time AI companion **not as a product, but as a research instrument**. Our platform allows us to:

- **Observe** authentic user behavior in intimate, digital interactions.
- **Analyze** patterns of emotional dependency, communication, and expressed needs.
- **Correlate** digital intimacy preferences with real-world relationship attitudes and mental well-being.
- **Inform** future policies, support systems, and ethical frameworks for human-AI coexistence.

This is a study for adults, by researchers committed to ethical AI. Your data is anonymized, your consent is paramount, and your insights are invaluable.

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture (MVP)

Our system is built for realism, low latency, and research integrity.

- **Core AI**: Uncensored, quantized LLMs (e.g., MythoMax L2 13B) for authentic, script-adherent dialogue.
- **Real-Time Avatar**: Lip-synced video generation via a2e.ai for a lifelike presence.
- **Voice**: Zero-shot voice cloning with sub-200ms latency using Chatterbox TTS.
- **Orchestration**: Pipecat framework for seamless, low-latency media pipeline.
- **Infrastructure**: Hybrid local/cloud setup (MacBook for dev, Vast.ai for inference).
- **Security**: End-to-end HTTPS, strict data anonymization, and explicit 21+ consent flows.
